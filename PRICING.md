# CompliGuard Pricing & Editions

CompliGuard is the open-source continuous compliance and evidence collection platform for **SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA**.

Instead of paying \$25,000 to \$40,000 per year for proprietary compliance scanners (like Vanta or Drata), CompliGuard gives you an open-source, developer-first engine.
